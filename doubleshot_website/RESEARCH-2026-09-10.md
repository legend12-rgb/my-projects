# DOUBLESHOT design research, 2026-09-10

The client asked for a 10/10 site, and for the design to be checked against what the
best sites actually do. Sources are listed at the bottom. Screenshots of every site
visited are in `creative-library/.playwright-mcp/research/`.

## What was inspected (live, in a real browser, with library detection)

| Site | Award | Stack found | What it does that we don't |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sons & Daughters | Awwwards SOTD + Developer, Feb 2025 | Lenis only, no GSAP, no WebGL | Full-bleed giant display type, photos kept SMALL and framed, lots of warm negative space |
| Sunbeam Bagels | Awwwards HM, Jul 2026 | GSAP + ScrollTrigger, 5 videos | Real interior video hero; **food shot as clean cut-outs on flat brand colour**; one colour per section; tilted photo strip |
| Café Binocle | Awwwards HM, Apr 2026 | GSAP + ScrollTrigger + SplitText + Lottie | Scrolling type marquee band, Lottie illustrations |
| Caffè Gilli | Awwwards HM, Aug 2026 | GSAP + ScrollTrigger | Hero photo inset with a margin (a frame), spaced uppercase serif statement |
| Tab Kitchen & Bakery | Awwwards HM, Feb 2026 | Framer | Branded loader, long editorial page |
| Onyx Coffee Lab | award-winning roaster | 15 videos | **Black-and-white portrait photography**: one treatment unifies uneven source photos; ticker bar |
| Blue Bottle | category reference | no GSAP | Calm product still life on concrete, huge whitespace, 01/02/03 slider |
| Rolex | the client's stated reference | no GSAP/WebGL | Full-bleed cinematic video, small spaced caps label + one big headline + one CTA, slow motion |
| La Colombe | category reference | 30 videos | Commerce-heavy; the pop-up and cookie-wall approach is what NOT to copy |

**Finding 1: nobody winning in this category uses WebGL or 3D.** The winners get their
premium feel from photography treatment, giant typography and restrained motion
(Lenis/GSAP). Our Three.js film is already more technically ambitious than any of them.
What we lack is their **editorial layout and consistent photography**.

**Finding 2: our photo problem is treatment, not quantity.** Research (Tablein, Moze
Creative, restaurant-photography sources) and the live sites agree that *inconsistent*
style, lighting and backgrounds make a restaurant look unprofessional. The winners fix
this one of three ways:
1. Cut-outs on flat colour (Sunbeam)
2. One monochrome/duotone grade across every photo (Onyx)
3. Few, small, framed photos with big type doing the work (Sons & Daughters, Gilli)

Our IG photos have steel counters, clutter, baked-in text and phone-camera colour, and
we show them large and raw. That is exactly the failure mode.

**Finding 3: scroll-motion principles** (Brad Holmes on Apple; the Apple teardown sources):
- Restraint.
- Separate light content motion from heavy visual motion.
- Pace by viewport, not by asset height.
- Test on mid-range Android.
- Apple now video-scrubs a single compressed MP4 rather than hundreds of frames.

Our 140-frame WebP sequence (7.8 MB desktop) is the older approach.

## Techniques worth adopting (all vanilla JS + GSAP from CDN, no build step)

- **Pinned named-list with synced image** (watched: "Awwwards-Level List Reveal Animation
  with GSAP ScrollTrigger", HankTheTank, 13 min). Left: list items. Right: one preview
  image. As you scroll the pinned section, the active item slides right and brightens,
  and the image swaps to that item's `data-image`. Lenis + ScrollTrigger. Direct fit for
  a menu: Jaggery latté, Masala chai latté, V60, Cold brew, each with its photo.
- **Layered zoom scroll** (Codrops, Oct 2025): 6 stacked copies of one image scaling
  0.15→1 with a 0.2s stagger and power1.inOut, pinned and scrubbed. Split text halves
  slide apart as the background scales. Candidate for the film-to-page handoff.
- **Scrolling type marquee** (Binocle, Onyx ticker): cheap, on-brand. "Sourcing · Roasting ·
  Brewing · 100% eggless · Amritsar".
- **Giant full-bleed display wordmark** (Sons & Daughters): DOUBLESHOT set edge to edge
  in the footer or at the end of the film.
- **21st.dev** parallax catalogue: "Zoom Parallax" (Efferd), "Scroll media expansion hero"
  (Arunachalam) and "Scroll Reveal Image" (unlumen) are the relevant patterns. They are
  React/Tailwind, so they would be re-implemented in vanilla JS, not installed.

## Our mistakes, ranked

1. Photos shown large, raw, with mixed backgrounds (steel, clutter, text overlays) and
   mixed colour. Biggest single cause of "unprofessional".
2. Motion is uniform (everything fades/wipes up). No signature interaction after the film.
3. Photos are reached only after ~8 screens of film scroll.
4. No typographic hero moment after the film. Winners use giant type as a design element.
5. The film is still a 7-cut AI montage (hard ceiling, unchanged).
6. Frame-sequence weight: 7.8 MB desktop, 3.4 MB mobile.

## Inner pages (second research pass, same day)

**Our state:** every inner page opens on a blurred film still + serif title + ~300px of
empty green. The menu is a plain two-column list whose hover image shows *random film
frames* (wrong for every item). The bakery shows a lone green veg icon and no food.
Locations is an identical 3-col card grid (banned by the taste doc). Motion is only char
reveals, and the page change is a plain swup fade.

**What winners do inside:**
- Sons & Daughters (about): giant type hero; full-width coloured bands that stack
  (position:sticky) with huge names.
- Dishoom: a story per location with photo, hours, transit and booking; list/map toggle.
- Caffè Gilli: inset framed full-width photography.
- Codrops "Async Page Transitions in Vanilla JS" (Feb 2026): the old page moves up and
  fades to 0.6 while the new one reveals via clip-path inset(100% 0 0 0)→inset(0),
  1s power2.inOut, with headings following at +0.45s expo.out. Works with swup 4 + GSAP.
- Cross-document View Transitions (`@view-transition { navigation: auto }`) give the same
  effect with zero JS on full reloads, which the homepage needs because its film can't be swup-swapped.
- Codrops "Animated Product Grid Preview" (May 2025): cross-shaped clip-path hover reveal,
  vanilla + GSAP. A candidate for the bakery counter.

**Concept proposed:**
- Global: wipe-up transition, giant-type heroes with a cut-out, marquee, giant footer.
- Menu: sticky category rail + featured cut-outs; hover images only where real.
- Roastery: pinned horizontal three-act scroll; brew methods as stacking bands.
- Bakery: floating cut-outs + an IG-sourced counter grid.
- Locations: an SVG map with lines drawn from the Amritsar roaster + expanding city rows.
